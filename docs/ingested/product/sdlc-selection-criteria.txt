Healthcare SDLC selection criteria.

Stable requirements and extensive approvals → Waterfall.
Patient-safety impact with strict verification and validation → V-Model.
High clinical, technical, privacy, or interoperability uncertainty → Spiral.
Frequently changing workflows and strong stakeholder feedback → Agile with explicit safety and security gates.
Continuous secure deployment and operational monitoring → DevSecOps.
Regulated healthcare with evolving requirements → named hybrid such as iterative delivery plus V-Model verification gates and DevSecOps controls.

Decision factors to extract from a specification before choosing:
requirement stability; patient-safety impact; regulatory and privacy criticality; security risk; project complexity; interoperability and legacy-system dependence; frequency of expected changes; need for continuous delivery; availability of clinicians and other stakeholders; testing and documentation requirements; budget and schedule constraints; need for formal verification; consequences of system failure.

Produce ranked recommendations with justification, then a project-specific workflow: phases, activities, roles, deliverables, security activities, testing, compliance checkpoints, human-approval gates, entry and exit criteria, traceability.
